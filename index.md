## Privacy Policy
 
_Last updated: October 2026_
 
### What we collect
- **Account data:** email address, display name, username and profile photo. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email from that provider.
- **Discoveries:** photos you choose to log, the identified species, date, and confidence of the identification.
- **Location:** when GPS is enabled, the location of each discovery (rounded coordinates and city/country). You can turn this off in Settings.
- **Social data:** friend requests, friendships, badges, XP and challenge progress.
 
### How we use it
Only to provide the app's features: your encyclopedia, profile, badges, daily challenges, friends and nearby sightings.
 
### On-device identification
Photos are analyzed on your device by the BioCLIP model. Identification does not send your photo to any server. A photo is uploaded only when you choose to log a discovery.
 
### Sharing
- Your discoveries, photos and profile are visible to your accepted friends.
- If enabled, your discoveries may appear in your friends' Nearby Sightings, shown as an approximate distance. Exact coordinates are never shown. You can disable this in Settings.
- We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.
 
### Service providers
- Supabase (database, authentication and file storage).
- Google and Apple, only if you use them to sign in.
 
### Third-party content
Species photos come from iNaturalist and descriptions from Wikipedia, under their respective Creative Commons licenses, with attribution shown in the app.
 
### Retention and deletion
Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from **Profile → Settings → Delete account**. This permanently removes your profile, discoveries, photos, badges and friendships. If you signed in with Apple, access is also revoked.
 
### Children
glassfrog is not directed to children under 13.
